DDoS Attacks

A Distributed Denial-of-Service (DDoS) attack is a malicious attempt to disrupt the normal traffic of a targeted server by overwhelming it with a flood of Internet traffic.

How to Identify

  • Look for thousands of simultaneous requests from the same IP or multiple IPs.
  • Watch for SYN_FLOOD, UDP_FLOOD, or excessive HTTP_GET logs in rapid succession.

Mitigation

The most immediate response is to Block IP at the firewall level for the offending source. In large-scale attacks, traffic must be routed through scrubbing centers or CDNs.

Phishing

Phishing is a social engineering attack where malicious actors deceive users into revealing sensitive information or installing malware, often by pretending to be a legitimate entity via email.

How to Identify

  • Look for SMTP_IN logs from external sources attempting to spoof internal domains.
  • Subject lines or payloads often mimic IT support, urgent invoices, or password reset alerts.

Mitigation

Deploy the Email Gateway tool to Quarantine the malicious email before the user clicks on any embedded links or opens attachments.
